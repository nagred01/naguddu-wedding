Privacy Policy

Bhavitha & Nagender's wedding website · naguddu.com

What we collect

When you RSVP, we save your name, your answers for each event (attending, number of guests, meal choice), and, only if you enter them, your email address, phone number and a note for us. Photos you upload on the Photos page are saved privately for the couple.

How we use it

Only to plan our wedding: counting guests and meals, and sending you a confirmation of your RSVP by email and/or text message if you gave us an email address or phone number.

Who can see it

Only Bhavitha and Nagender. Your details are kept in a private Google Sheet. Text messages are sent through Twilio and emails through Google, only to deliver your confirmation.

Text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

We delete RSVP details after the wedding celebrations are over.

Your choices

Email and phone number are optional. Reply STOP to any text to stop messages. To have your details removed, reply to your confirmation email or contact Bhavitha or Nagender directly.
