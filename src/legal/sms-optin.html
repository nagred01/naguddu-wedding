How guests opt in to RSVP texts

Bhavitha & Nagender's wedding · naguddu.com

Guests open naguddu.com, tap RSVP, and fill in the RSVP form. The phone number box is optional. Directly under it the form shows:

"By entering your phone number, you agree to receive one RSVP confirmation text from Bhavitha & Nagender. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help." with links to the SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

Only guests who type a phone number and tap Send RSVP receive a single confirmation text listing their answers. No marketing messages are sent.
