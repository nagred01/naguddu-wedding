Bhavitha & Nagender's wedding RSVP texts · naguddu.com

What you'll receive

When you RSVP on naguddu.com and choose to enter your phone number, we send you one text message confirming your RSVP: which wedding events you said you'll attend and how many guests. If you update your RSVP later, you get one new confirmation. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

How you opt in

Giving your phone number is optional. You opt in by typing your number into the RSVP form, which shows this notice next to the phone box: "By entering your phone number, you agree to receive one RSVP confirmation text from Bhavitha & Nagender. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

Message frequency

One message per RSVP you submit.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You'll get one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed. Reply HELP for help.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. We never sell or share your phone number.
