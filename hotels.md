# Hotel shortlist

**On the site now** (wedding assistant → "Where should I stay?", list in `src/i18n.ts` → `HOTELS`):
the four McKinney hotels and Holiday Inn Express & Suites Prosper. Not listed yet: the Anna hotel
(opening unconfirmed) and Comfort Suites McKinney - Allen (address unconfirmed).

Venues:
- Haldi, Mehendi, Pellikuthuru / Pellikoduku: Serengeti Events (Airbnb), 6147 FM 455, Anna, TX 75409

between Anna (north) and Fairview (south), so it is the easiest single base for the week.
